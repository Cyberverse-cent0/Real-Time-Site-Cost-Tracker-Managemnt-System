🏗️ Real-Time Site Cost Tracker & Management System
A real-time construction site cost tracking and management platform designed to give project managers, site engineers, and stakeholders live visibility into every dollar spent on a construction project — from labor and materials to equipment and subcontractors.

📌 Overview
Managing construction costs is notoriously difficult: expenses pile up across multiple sites, vendors, and teams, and by the time reports are compiled, the budget is already blown. This system solves that by capturing cost data as it happens and presenting it through a real-time dashboard with alerts, analytics, and reporting.

✨ Key Features
Real-Time Cost Dashboard – Live updates on total spend, remaining budget, and cost-per-category as expenses are logged.

Budget Management – Set budgets per project, phase, or cost center with automatic threshold alerts.

Expense Tracking – Log labor, materials, equipment, subcontractor, and miscellaneous costs on the fly.

Multi-Site Support – Manage multiple construction sites from a single interface with per-site breakdowns.

Role-Based Access Control – Distinct views and permissions for admins, project managers, site engineers, and finance teams.

Alerts & Notifications – Instant warnings when spending approaches or exceeds budget limits.

Reports & Analytics – Generate daily, weekly, and monthly cost reports; visualize trends with charts.

Audit Trail – Every entry is timestamped and traceable for accountability and compliance.

Offline-First Sync (optional) – Field-friendly data entry that syncs when connectivity is restored.
